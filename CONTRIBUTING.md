# Contributing

This is a demonstration project. I keep the demo working in current Stata, but I'm not taking feature requests, and I may take a long time to review a pull request.

If the demo is broken or a step in the README doesn't work as described, feel free to open an issue. I read them when I can, and I don't promise a response.

Never upload confidential data, restricted manuscripts, direct identifiers, credentials, or unpublished research logic. Reproduce problems with the included synthetic data or another fully synthetic example.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be distributed under the repository's license.
